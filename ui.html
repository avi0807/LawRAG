Agent Pipeline
1
OrchestratorIdentifies legal entities (cases, articles, judges) from your query and decides retrieval strategy — vector, graph, or hybrid.
2
RetrieverSearches Qdrant for semantically similar judgment chunks and traverses the knowledge graph up to 2 hops for related cases and articles. FlashRank reranks results.
3
CriticChecks if retrieved evidence is legally sufficient. If not, expands the query and retries retrieval up to 2 times.
4
GeneratorSynthesizes a cited answer grounded strictly in retrieved judgments and constitutional provisions, with a confidence score.
